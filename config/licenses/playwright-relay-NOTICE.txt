Browser Workbench kernel relay protocol adaptation

The tab/CDP protocol translation in src/infrastructure/kernel-relay-model.ts
follows the design of Microsoft's Playwright BrowserModel:
https://github.com/microsoft/playwright/blob/606ecd8b34bac100db32561b6278ffd76e5eb3cd/packages/playwright-core/src/tools/mcp/browserModel.ts

Workbench changes: strict JSON contracts, owned-tab and child-session scope,
private loopback SDK endpoint, fail-closed browser/method/file policy,
connection-generation and lease fences, bounded stop confirmation and
uncertain-input evidence. The SDK itself is installed unmodified.

and third-party notices. These notices do not imply that Microsoft endorses
or has validated Browser Workbench or its Facebook task implementation.
